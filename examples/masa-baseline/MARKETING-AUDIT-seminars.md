## Executive Summary

masaseminars.com is a purpose-built seminar registration site — MASA's primary B2C acquisition channel — scoring 47/100. The site has adequate trust signals and a clear value proposition (free lunch-and-learn), but is functionally broken at its core conversion moment: the events page currently shows **zero events**. Every visitor who clicks "Find an event" — the primary CTA — encounters an empty calendar. This is the equivalent of a retail store with a "Come in!" sign on the door and nothing on the shelves.

Beyond the empty events issue, the site is a conversion dead-end for visitors outside a seminar area or between event cycles: there is no "can't attend? buy online" path to masaaccess.com, no email nurture beyond a notification list, and no digital fallback for the 18 states where MASA membership is unavailable. The site is essentially a one-function microsite that fails at its one function when no events are scheduled.

The trust signals are genuinely strong: ABC/NBC/CBS media mentions, BBB logo, Google Reviews, Facebook, and "2 million members" are above-average for this product category. These could support a much higher-converting funnel with structural fixes.

## MASA-Specific Observations

- **The seminar channel is MASA's primary acquisition mechanism** — this site is not an experiment, it is the pipeline. A D-grade here has direct revenue impact.
- **The empty events page is an emergency** — not a medium-priority issue. Visitors who arrived specifically to find a seminar now have no path forward except a phone call or a notification form. Both are high-friction and low-conversion.
- **No "buy online" bridge from this site to masaaccess.com** — seminar visitors who are ready to buy but can't find a local event are lost entirely. A simple "No event in your area? You can join MASA online" link to masaaccess.com/compare-plans/ would recapture this traffic.
- **State availability is buried** — 18 states where MASA is not available are disclosed only in fine print. A visitor from New York, Massachusetts, or Washington who goes through the entire event-finding flow only to discover MASA isn't available there has a terrible experience.
- **The site's purpose is narrow but important** — do not over-engineer it. It needs to do one thing well: get qualified prospects to register for a seminar or bridge them to an online enrollment path if no seminar is available.

## Critical Issues

### C1 — Events page shows zero active events
**URL:** https://www.masaseminars.com/events
**Impact:** This is the terminal destination of every CTA on the site. Visitors who click "Find an event" or "RSVP now" see "No Event Found." There is no explanation of when events will resume, no timeline, no indication of whether the calendar is actively maintained.
**Fix:**
- If events are genuinely paused: add a banner explaining the pause and directing visitors to masaaccess.com for online enrollment
- If events exist but aren't loading: urgent technical investigation
- Always show the "Get event notifications" form prominently when no results are found (it exists but may not be prominent enough)
- Add a "Can't find an event? Join MASA online" CTA linking to masaaccess.com/compare-plans/ on every zero-results state

### C2 — No bridge from masaseminars.com to online enrollment
**Impact:** A visitor who finds this site, reads about MASA's value proposition, and wants to enroll but can't find a local event has no path to purchase. The site's only conversion options are (1) attend a seminar or (2) wait for notification. Neither is available when there are no events.
**Fix:** Add a persistent CTA across the site: "Prefer to enroll online? Visit masaaccess.com/compare-plans/" — especially on the events page zero-results state and in the FAQ ("Can I join without attending a seminar?").

## High Priority Issues

### H1 — No meta description on homepage
**Impact:** Every organic search result for masaseminars.com shows auto-generated snippet text. Reduces click-through rate from search.
**Fix:** Add: "MASA offers free lunch-and-learn seminars on emergency medical transportation coverage. Find an event near you — no cost to attend."

### H2 — FAQ answers are too brief for conversion
11 FAQ questions covering attendance logistics — but none that answer the key prospect question: "What does MASA actually cost and what does it cover?" The FAQ serves attendees, not prospects.
**Fix:** Add 3–5 product-level FAQs: "What does MASA membership cost?", "What does MASA cover?", "How is MASA different from health insurance?", "Can I join MASA without attending a seminar?"

### H3 — No presenter credentials or bio
The About-us page confirms seminars are led by MASA representatives, but names no one. Testimonials reference "the presenter" generically.
**Fix:** Add presenter bios with names, photos, and relevant background (insurance background, emergency services experience, years with MASA). This increases trust and makes the event feel less like a sales pitch.

### H4 — State availability not surfaced upfront
18 states are excluded. A visitor from an excluded state who goes through the RSVP flow only to discover MASA isn't available there will feel deceived.
**Fix:** Add a "Check coverage availability in your state" tool or a clear note above the event search: "MASA is not currently available in AK, DE, DC, KY, IA, MA, MD, MI, MT, NH, NM, ND, NY, NJ, PA, SC, UT, WA, WV, RI."

### H5 — No social sharing or referral mechanism for seminar invitations
Testimonials show attendees bring spouses and friends. There is no "invite a friend" or social sharing feature for confirmed registrants.
**Fix:** Add an "Invite a guest" email share option in the confirmation/RSVP flow.

## Strategic Recommendations

1. **Build a "Buy online" path as a permanent secondary CTA across the site.** The seminar is the preferred acquisition channel, but there should always be a fallback to online enrollment. "Prefer to skip the seminar? Plans from $63/month at masaaccess.com."

2. **Add a post-RSVP email sequence.** Confirmed registrants should receive: (1) confirmation with event details, (2) reminder 3 days before, (3) day-before reminder with "what to bring / what to expect," (4) post-event follow-up with enrollment link. Currently, what happens after RSVP is unknown.

3. **Create a "seminar experience" page.** Prospects are being asked to give up 60–90 minutes and travel to an unfamiliar venue. A page with photos, video clips of seminar presentations, attendee testimonials with specific quotes about the experience (not just the coverage), and a clear agenda would dramatically improve RSVP conversion from uncertain prospects.

4. **Add Event schema to all event listings.** This enables Google to show events in search results and AI-powered local search features — directly increasing seminar attendance from organic discovery. (See GEO audit for full schema spec.)

5. **Cross-link masaseminars.com ↔ masaaccess.com strategically.** masaaccess.com should reference seminars as a way to learn more ("Want a personal walkthrough? Find a seminar near you"). masaseminars.com should reference online enrollment as a fallback. Currently these sites operate in silos.
